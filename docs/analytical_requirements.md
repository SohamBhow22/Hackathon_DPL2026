# Analytical Requirements — KKR IPL Performance Analysis

## 1. Objective

The objective of this project is to analyse Kolkata Knight Riders' 
performance across the 2023 and 2024 IPL seasons to identify:

1. The team's principal strengths and weaknesses.
2. The players, roles, and performance characteristics that 
   contributed to successful and unsuccessful outcomes.
3. The phases and match situations in which KKR gained or lost 
   competitive advantage.
4. The player profiles that could address identified performance 
   gaps and improve team composition.
5. The tactical and strategic adjustments that KKR could consider 
   based on the available evidence.

The analysis will progress from descriptive statistics to diagnostic 
investigation and, where supported by the data, strategic recommendations.

Every major finding must connect to at least one of the following:

- Team performance evaluation
- Player performance evaluation
- Player-role assessment
- Scouting and recruitment profiles
- Match strategy and tactical decisions

The analysis must distinguish observed patterns from causal explanations.
Recommendations must acknowledge the limitations of the available data.

## 2. Analytical Questions

### 2.1 Team Performance

- How did KKR perform across the two seasons?
- What characteristics distinguish their wins from their losses?
- Which strengths and weaknesses persisted across both seasons?
- Which observations appear specific to an individual season?

### 2.2 Batting Performance

- How did KKR generate runs across different innings phases?
- Where did the team lose wickets or struggle to maintain scoring?
- How was batting contribution distributed across players?
- Which batting roles or performance profiles appear most valuable?
- What batting characteristics could address identified weaknesses?

### 2.3 Bowling Performance

- How effectively did KKR restrict scoring and take wickets?
- How did bowling performance differ across innings phases?
- Were weaknesses concentrated among particular players, roles, 
  or match situations?
- Which bowling characteristics were associated with successful outcomes?
- What bowling profiles could address identified weaknesses?

### 2.4 Phase Performance

- How did KKR perform in the Powerplay, middle overs, and death overs?
- In which phases did KKR consistently gain or surrender an advantage?
- Were phase-level weaknesses associated with particular player roles?
- What role-specific adjustments could improve performance?

### 2.5 Player Roles and Impact

- Which players contributed most effectively within their roles?
- How did player performance vary across match situations?
- Are high aggregate statistics driven by volume, efficiency, 
  or contextual opportunities?
- Which players performed complementary roles?
- What player characteristics distinguish stronger performances 
  within comparable roles?

### 2.6 Scouting and Player Profiles

- Which performance gaps could potentially be addressed through recruitment?
- What measurable player characteristics would address each gap?
- Which characteristics can be evaluated using the available dataset?
- Which scouting questions cannot be answered using this dataset alone?
- How can we distinguish a genuine recruitment need from a problem 
  that could be addressed through role allocation or tactics?

### 2.7 Match Strategy

- How did KKR's performance differ when batting first versus chasing?
- Which measurable match characteristics were associated with winning?
- Were certain player roles or performance patterns particularly 
  valuable in specific match situations?
- Which tactical adjustments are supported by recurring evidence?

## 3. Connecting Performance Findings to Player Profiles

Every major performance weakness should be investigated through 
the following sequence:

1. Identify the observed performance gap.
2. Determine when and under what conditions it occurs.
3. Investigate which existing players and roles are associated 
   with the gap.
4. Identify measurable player characteristics that could address it.
5. Determine whether the issue is potentially related to player 
   capability, role allocation, or match strategy.
6. Define the player profile or tactical adjustment that could 
   address the issue.
7. State the evidence supporting the recommendation and its limitations.

### Example

Observed gap:
KKR concede a high proportion of runs during the death overs.

Diagnostic questions:
- Is the problem excessive boundary scoring, insufficient wickets, 
  or both?
- Is the weakness concentrated among particular bowlers?
- Does it persist across both seasons?
- Does performance vary with the match situation?

Potential player-profile implication:
Identify the measurable bowling characteristics associated with 
better death-over performance and determine whether those 
characteristics represent a potential scouting requirement.

Potential strategic implication:
Evaluate whether existing bowlers could be deployed differently 
or whether a different bowling profile may be needed.

Important limitation:
The analysis may identify desirable player characteristics, but 
it cannot establish that a particular real-world recruit would 
improve KKR without additional evidence.

## 4. Evidence and Interpretation Standards

1. Descriptive findings must be supported by calculated metrics 
   or visualisations.
2. Diagnostic explanations must be investigated rather than assumed.
3. Comparisons must account for relevant differences in player roles, 
   innings phases, and match situations where the data permits.
4. Findings should be checked across seasons where appropriate.
5. Association must not be presented as causation.
6. Recommendations must distinguish between evidence, interpretation, 
   and assumptions.
7. Scouting recommendations must identify measurable player 
   characteristics rather than rely solely on player rankings.
8. Metrics must use clearly defined formulas and appropriate 
   analytical grains.
9. Missing values, incomplete matches, and other known data 
   limitations must be handled explicitly.
10. No conclusion should require information that is unavailable 
    in the permitted dataset unless that limitation is clearly stated.

## 5. Analytical Scope

The analysis uses the organiser-provided IPL delivery-level dataset 
covering the 2023 and 2024 seasons.

KKR's 2024 Qualifier 1 and Final are included. Their final two 
league matches are absent from the supplied records.

The analysis will not assume that absent matches represent losses 
or infer unobserved performance.

The raw dataset will remain unchanged. All transformations, metrics, 
and analytical outputs must be reproducible.
